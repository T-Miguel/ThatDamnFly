# Causal report, supplementary: body v1.3 at level 3 (the "nervous" fly)

**Not confirmatory** (protocol v1.1 fixes level 0). It serves to check that the causal effect of the circuit persists when the wandering is at level 3 (4th fly): base speed 0.46 W·s⁻¹, frequent darts.

**Reading:** the reduction by ablation holds (median R 1.06; 95 % of the conditions) and the fly keeps moving away (D_away ≥ 0.05 and flee ≥ 0.65 in every direction). The lateralization index (LI) falls below 0.30 in several directions - **an artefact of the metric**: the LI compares the "side of the threat" with the fly's *initial* heading, and at level 3 the heading changes several times during the stimulus (darts with 60–180° turns), so "ipsi/contra" is no longer well defined. There is no evidence of loss of the causal effect; there is evidence that the LI needs to be computed with the instantaneous heading for agile flies (future protocol improvement, v1.2).

## Limitations (inferred)

- The model resolves left/right (hemispheres), not front/back: the DN readout does not distinguish sectors 1 vs 3 of the same hemisphere; 180° is not a criterion.
- Per-sector retinotopy (H1) is assigned, not measured; the MaleCNS flat files carry no receptive fields.
- L/R reconstruction asymmetry (LC4 71 L vs 55 R; LC4→DNp04 6,811 vs 4,786 synapses) compensated only in the input gain (p2); it persists in the weights.
- LIF parameters/scale/inputs are choices calibrated on a frontal stimulus; they are not biophysical measurements.
- Ablation B removes every direct VPN→DN edge; indirect VPN→interneuron→DN paths remain - the residual response in B is real and reported.
- The virtual body is a stylized kinematic model; the wandering (H6) is a stochastic process independent of threats, identical across arms.
- The wandering (H6) drifts forward (0.18 W·s⁻¹): per direction, arms B/C/E show a direction-dependent baseline of D_away (negative for frontal threats, positive for rear ones). That is why R can exceed 1 and why the ablated body "walks into the threat" frontally - it is not attraction.
- No claim of a complete brain, consciousness, pain, learning or biological validation of the escape.
