# Provenance of the identity

Direction approved by the owner in conversation: Concept 01, That Damn Fly. The board was generated with the integrated image generation tool from an original project brief. The approved image used light paper, a yellow THAT plate, a dominant DAMN FLY and a fly on the Y. A later raster extraction was not used as a production original.

Brand Kit v1 materializes the direction by vector reconstruction: own Bézier geometry for the fly; Archivo Black typography as outlines, with adjusted scale and spacing; flat colour fields. It uses no raster images hidden in SVG nor cut-outs of the board as the final logo. The paper texture is not part of the transparent resources. It contains no third-party symbols and claims no legal exclusivity of the mark.

Font: Archivo Black, Omnibus-Type. Original obtained from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/archivoblack/ArchivoBlack-Regular.ttf`, together with `OFL.txt` from the same directory. SHA-256 of the TTF: `dd9a89a019b4849f66ab75455fe7bdf931311042cbb0f0f97acc061539703180`. SIL OFL 1.1 license; see the full text included. The original font was not modified; the outlines used in the drawing are art derived from the typographic composition. Preserve the license when redistributing the TTF.

The package scripts are construction and export tools. fontTools and sharp are development dependencies, not libraries the game has to ship. If they are incorporated in the project chain, inventory their versions and licenses. The art uses no scientific data nor components of the neural core.

Visual brief of the board: a strong two-line logotype, THAT on a small yellow field above DAMN FLY; an original dark fly, two brick-coloured eyes, two light wings and six legs; a horizontal variant and a yellow icon; simple editorial humour, without weapons, blood or neural circuits. The vector refinement fixes one single fly for all applications and removes textures to scale cleanly.
