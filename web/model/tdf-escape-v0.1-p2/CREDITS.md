This game uses a circuit **selected and adapted** from a male specimen: 816 neurons and 38,521 connections chosen around the LC4/LPLC2/LPLC1/LPLC4 → descending neuron pathways (DNp01 "Giant Fiber", DNp02, DNp03, DNp04, DNp06, DNp11). Indices, scales, synaptic signs (by predicted neurotransmitter) and simulation parameters were changed; the per-sector retinotopy and the motor mapping are simulator hypotheses. The data authors do not sponsor and have not validated this game.
